WHO WE ARE

Two identities

From tCds to RD Design, the design story continues.

The Contemporary Design Studio (tCds)

Over 20 years of multidisciplinary design leadership.

Prior to establishing R D Design (RdD), Ruchi Dhillon was a partner and design lead for over 20 years at The Contemporary Design Studio (tCds), a multidisciplinary practice.

That chapter formed part of more than three decades of her experience as a design lead on projects of varying scales across the region. Following the closure of tCds in 2019, the team and ongoing projects transitioned seamlessly to RdD.

Partner & Design Lead • Multidisciplinary Practice • Transitioned in 2019

R D Design (RdD)

A leading architecture and interior design practice based in Colombo, Sri Lanka.

Founded in 2019 by Ruchi Dhillon, a Chartered Architect, RdD was conceived as a more agile platform for delivering design-led architecture and interiors.

Projects are developed through carefully curated, project-specific collaborations, integrating specialised expertise as needed to deliver efficient, responsive and cost-effective outcomes while maintaining a strong commitment to design, quality and timelines.

The transition from tCds reflects continued growth, renewed vision and a commitment to exceptional design and innovation. All ongoing projects and collaborations moved seamlessly under R D Design (RdD), allowing the team to serve clients with the same dedication and creativity, now with expanded capabilities and a fresh perspective.

Founded 2019 • Colombo • Chartered Architect • Design • Quality • Timelines
